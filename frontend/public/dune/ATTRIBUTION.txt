Dune: Awakening admin map images
================================

hagga-basin.webp, deepdesert.webp, arrakeen.webp, harko.webp

Sourced from the Sergentval pelican-egg-dune-awakening admin SPA
(https://github.com/Sergentval/pelican-egg-dune-awakening), which
lifted them from Icehunter/dune-admin (MIT). World-coordinate frames live
in frontend/src/lib/duneMapCoords.ts and must change with the images.

arrakeen.webp and harko.webp are cropped. Upstream letterboxes both into a
512 x 512 canvas (Arrakeen's content is the top 512 x 292, Harko's the
top-left 320 x 320) and applies the world bounds across the whole square, so
the bundled originals showed wide black bars. They ship cropped to their
content with minX/maxX/minY/maxY shrunk by the same fraction, which leaves
every world-to-pixel projection identical to upstream's. hagga-basin.webp is
upstream's 4096 px original downscaled to 512; deepdesert.webp is unmodified.
Re-cropping means recomputing the bounds and bumping the ?v= cache-buster.

Dune: Awakening is copyright Funcom. These images are bundled only so
operators can place last-saved player positions on the in-game maps.
